What's included, what's extra

Standard laser engraving, foil monogramming and hand-lettering are built into the item's shelf price — no surprise fees at the counter. Add-on pricing only applies to: deep metal engraving (+$5–$10), embroidery on customer-supplied items (+$8 flat), and rush same-hour turnaround on orders of 5+ pieces (+$15). Everything is quoted before we start.

Corporate & bulk gifting

Client thank-yous, employee milestones, holiday client gifts — we batch-engrave and wrap 10+ pieces with consistent placement across the whole order. Invoice billing available for local businesses. Reach out at least a week ahead of your date so we can schedule studio time.
