Our Story

A little storefront with a big card wall.

Ribbon & Twine opened on Willow Creek Lane in 2011 with one simple idea: gift-giving should feel personal, and it should never require overnight shipping just to say "I'm thinking of you."

Where It Started

Three generations, one counter.

Founder Marisol Calloway started Ribbon & Twine out of a single rented room, hand-wrapping gifts for neighbors who didn't want to drive forty minutes to the nearest mall. Today her daughter Elena runs the shop floor, and Marisol still teaches the engraving studio on weekends.

What hasn't changed is the promise behind the counter: every card is chosen thoughtfully, every gift is wrapped by hand, and if you need something today, we'll do everything we can to have it ready before you finish your errands.

Why We're Pickup-First

Because "the party's tonight" is a real problem.

Shipping a card takes days. Forgetting a gift takes seconds to realize and hours to fix — unless there's a shop nearby that can wrap it while you wait.

No shipping cutoffs

Reserve at noon, hold it in your hands by 2pm — no carrier delays to worry about.

A human helps you choose

Our staff know the card wall by heart and will help you find the right words.

Money stays in Maple Hollow

Shopping local keeps our small downtown block full of neighbors, not warehouses.

In-Store Experience

Come in, we'll wrap it while you decide what to write.

Walk past the card wall, browse the occasion tables, and stop by the studio counter where you can watch your gift get engraved. Kids love picking their own cards; last-minute shoppers love that we never make them feel rushed.

  • Free coffee & cocoa at the counter, October–March
  • Kid-height card rack so little ones can pick their own
  • Watch your item engraved through the studio window
