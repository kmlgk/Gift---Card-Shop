📦 Pickup & Ordering

Reserving ahead, hold times, and how delivery works.

Reserve your gifts online or by phone, and we'll prepare, wrap and hold them at the counter under your name. Orders placed by 2pm are typically ready the same day.

We're a pickup-first shop. For Maple Hollow residents who truly can't make it in, ask about local hand-delivery — available for a small fee within a five-mile radius.

Reserved orders are held for 7 days. We'll send a reminder if it's getting close, and we're happy to extend it — just give us a call.

🎁 Wrapping & Personalization

Free wrap on every order, plus engraving proofs and timing.

Yes — hand-wrapping is included on every purchase, in-store or reserved online, with a choice of paper and ribbon color.

Most engraving and monogramming is finished in about two hours. See our full turnaround chart by material and method.

Always. We'll show you a quick digital mock-up of the text and placement so you can approve it before anything is engraved.

↩️ Returns & Exchanges

Our 14-day policy, and what happens if we get an engraving wrong.

Unpersonalized items can be returned within 14 days with a receipt for store credit or exchange. Personalized and engraved items are final sale, since they're made just for you.

If we made the error against your approved proof, we'll remake it free of charge, as quickly as materials allow.

🏢 Corporate & Bulk Gifting

Office holiday gifts, client thank-yous, and volume timing.

Yes — many Maple Hollow businesses order client and staff gifts through us every year. We offer volume pricing on 10+ identical items and can batch personalization for consistency.

At least one week for orders under 25 items, two weeks during the November–December holiday rush.

Yes, for established local businesses ordering 10 or more items — ask at the counter or email us to set up net-15 invoice billing.

🏬 Visiting the Shop

Hours, parking, and what to expect when you walk in.

Yes — free street parking runs the length of Willow Creek Lane, and there's a small free lot behind the building, accessible from Birch Street.

Yes — a ramped entrance, wide aisles, and a kid-height section of the card wall so little ones can browse comfortably too.

We follow our regular Mon–Sat 9am–7pm, Sun 11am–4pm hours right up through December 24th, and we're closed only on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day.
